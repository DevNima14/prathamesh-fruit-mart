About Prathamesh Fruit Mart

Located near Bank of India in Dattawadi, Pune, Prathamesh Fruit Mart is your neighbourhood shop for fresh, high-quality fruits. Every fruit we sell is sourced directly from trusted farms and carefully selected for quality and hygiene.

From daily essentials like bananas and apples to seasonal specials like mangoes and oranges, we make sure you always get freshness, great taste, and fair prices. ताजे फळे, रुचकर दर! (Fresh fruits, fair prices!)

We also serve bulk and wholesale buyers — hotels, juice shops, caterers, and retailers — with dependable supply and consistent quality.
